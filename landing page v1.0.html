Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are these 3D models used for in practice? +
These models serve three main purposes: (1) Sales engineers use them in client presentations to explain complex system configurations visually; (2) Engineering teams use them for layout proposals and design review; (3) Training managers use them to explain system operation to operators and technical staff. The models are not decorative — they are working visualization tools for technical communication.
Are these models based on real industrial systems? +
Yes. All models are constructed from real engineering data including actual skid dimensions, standard membrane configurations, real component placements, and typical P&I layouts used in the water treatment industry. The RO systems, filtration skids, and wastewater plants reflect how these systems are actually built and operated.
What file formats are included? +
Most models include OBJ, FBX, and GLB formats. Some also include 3DS and native formats. PBR texture maps (albedo, roughness, metalness, normal) are included with all models. This covers compatibility with Blender, 3ds Max, Maya, Cinema 4D, Unreal Engine, and most real-time viewers.
Can I use these in a commercial sales presentation? +
Yes. Both Fab.com and CGTrader offer commercial licensing options that allow use in client presentations, project proposals, marketing materials, and internal documentation. Check the specific license on each platform for exact terms. The models are specifically designed for this B2B use case.
Is there a model that doesn't fit my project capacity? +
If you need a model at a capacity or configuration not currently available (for example, 100 m³/h, or a specific process layout), custom model requests can be submitted. Contact through the platforms or reach out directly. Custom renders and animation sequences can also be arranged for specific project needs.
Do the models work in Unreal Engine or Unity? +
Yes. GLB and FBX formats are compatible with Unreal Engine 5, Unity, and other real-time platforms. Models purchased through Fab.com have direct pipeline compatibility with Unreal Engine. The PBR materials are set up for both metalness/roughness and specular/glossiness workflows.
