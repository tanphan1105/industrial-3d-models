HUONG DAN DONG BO VA CHAY FILE ILOGIC TRONG INVENTOR:
===================================================

De chay cac file rule (.vb) nay ma khong can copy-paste vao tung file Assembly cua Inventor, ban hay thiet lap chay duoi dang "External Rule" nhu sau:

Buoc 1: Them thu muc chua Rule vao Inventor
1. Mo Autodesk Inventor len.
2. Mo tab "Manage" tren thanh Ribbon.
3. Click chon "iLogic Browser" de hien thi bang dieu khien iLogic.
4. Tren bang iLogic Browser, click vao tab "External Rules".
5. Chuot phai vao khoang trong cua tab "External Rules" -> chon "Configure iLogic Options".
6. Tai o "External Rule Directories", click vao nut dau "+" va chon duong dan den thu muc:
   D:\WT3D_Project\Z_Tools
7. Nhan OK. Ban se thay 2 file "AlignToOrigin.vb" va "SetCenterDistance.vb" xuat hien trong danh sach.

Buoc 2: Test Rule 1 - AlignToOrigin.vb (Can tim ve Goc va Khoa cung)
1. Tao hoac mo 1 Assembly moi (.iam).
2. Chen (Place) mot chi tiet bat ky vao va dat lech tam.
3. Click chon chi tiet do tren man hinh (no se duoc highlight xanh).
4. O tab "External Rules", click chuot phai vao rule "AlignToOrigin" -> chon "Run Rule".
5. Chi tiet se tu dong bay ve tam va duoc khoa Grounded.

Buoc 3: Test Rule 2 - SetCenterDistance.vb (Dat khoang cach Tim - Tim)
1. Chen 2 chi tiet vao ban ve Assembly.
2. Giu Ctrl va click chon ca 2 chi tiet nay tren man hinh.
3. O tab "External Rules", click chuot phai vao rule "SetCenterDistance" -> chon "Run Rule".
4. Nhap khoang cach (vi du 500) vao o thong bao va nhan Enter.
5. Hai chi tiet tu dong dich chuyen theo dung tam YZ ve khoang cach 500mm.
