FAQ

Common Questions

What industrial 3D models are available? +
RO systems (10, 30, 50 m³/h), multimedia filtration skids, activated carbon filter units, UF membrane systems, complete wastewater treatment plants (primary + secondary + tertiary stages), and industrial MCC control panels with PLC/HMI components.
Are the RO system models based on real engineering specs? +
Yes. All models are constructed from real-world engineering data: actual skid dimensions, standard membrane element counts (4040 / 8040 elements), valve positions per P&ID conventions, TDS/pressure/flow sensor placements, and antiscalant dosing configurations used in actual water treatment projects across SEA and the Middle East.
What file formats are included? +
OBJ, FBX, GLB, and 3DS formats. Full PBR texture maps included: albedo, roughness, metalness, and normal maps in 2K/4K. Compatible with Blender, 3ds Max, Maya, Cinema 4D, Unreal Engine 5, Unity, and all standard real-time viewers including Sketchfab.
Can I use these in a commercial client presentation? +
Yes. Commercial licenses available on both Fab.com and CGTrader cover use in client presentations, technical proposals, marketing materials, training programs, and internal documentation. These models are specifically designed and tested for B2B engineering sales workflows.
Can I request a custom RO system model at a different capacity? +
Yes. Custom model requests are accepted for capacities and configurations not in the current catalog — including 100 m³/h+, specific process configurations, seawater desalination (SWRO) skids, or custom skid dimensions. Contact via Zalo (0985267326) or email trongtan.p@icloud.com for timeline and pricing.
Do these models work in Unreal Engine 5? +
Yes. GLB and FBX formats are compatible with Unreal Engine 5 and Unity. Models purchased on Fab.com have direct UE5 Marketplace pipeline integration. PBR materials use metalness/roughness workflow compatible with UE5's material system and Lumen lighting.
