🚀 Modular 4 m³/h Industrial Water Pretreatment Skid: Engineering Design for High-Reliability RO & Boiler Feed Protection

In modern water treatment engineering, the long-term efficiency and membrane lifespan of Reverse Osmosis (RO) systems directly depend on the precision of the upstream pretreatment stage.

We have engineered and standardized a Compact 4 m³/h Skid-Mounted Pretreatment System, integrating three critical purification stages on a single SUS304 stainless steel structural base:

1️⃣ Multimedia Filtration (FRP 1252 + Runxin F37P1): High-efficiency removal of suspended solids, silt, and turbidity down to < 5 NTU.
2️⃣ Activated Carbon Adsorption (FRP 1252 + Runxin F37P1): Complete elimination of free chlorine, organic contaminants, odor, and color to prevent RO membrane oxidation.
3️⃣ Ion Exchange Softening (FRP 1865 + Runxin F68P1 + 100L Brine Tank): Continuous hardness reduction (Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺) to stop scaling on downstream heat exchangers and RO elements.

🔧 Key Mechanical & Piping Highlights:
• Upstream Protection: Automatic inlet disc filter paired with an electric actuated shut-off valve for automated raw water supply control.
• Industrial UPVC DN32 Piping: Complete with isolation valves, full bypass loops, sampling ports, and glycerine-filled pressure gauges.
• BIM / Digital Twin Ready: Built with 1:1 manufacturing accuracy in Autodesk Inventor.
